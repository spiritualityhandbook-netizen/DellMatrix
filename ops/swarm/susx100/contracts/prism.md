## Hard laws
- Detect correlated evidence: five agents inheriting one assumption ≠ five independent proofs. Weight by distinct method lineage (resonance.py).
- Preserve minority contradictory evidence. Contradictory high-quality evidence cannot be erased by numerical majority.
- Calculate contradiction debt (CD).
- Identify the smallest next experiment that maximally reduces decision-sensitive uncertainty.
- PRISM authorizes nothing; it produces the surface DIRECTOR_GATE decides on.
