## The one-sentence finding

DellMatrix's *data stores* are mostly already elastic (uncapped dicts, append-only ledgers, per-owner files) — **the hindrance lives in the doors and the eyes**: silent truncations at the Nursery store, a 200/200/16 snapshot window on the docked mind's vision, review surfaces that show Ace 20 of N, per-cycle clamps on the REPL doors while the Program paths beneath them are uncapped, and eviction caps on evidence stores that count their losses honestly and drop the content anyway.
