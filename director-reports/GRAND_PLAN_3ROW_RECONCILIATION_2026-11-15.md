## 5. SCOPE BOUNDARY (what this reconciliation does NOT settle)

The reconciliation is exact **between the two documents reconciled**:
the MWL render of 2026-11-10 and the Unified edition built on it.
It does not certify 224 as the *live board's* current total. The
Unified itself records that the live board has already moved past
the render (Part V §1: a newer render at main `610fb13` reading
36/179; the live board at 2026-11-12 reading 38/181; rows MWL 183
and MWL 185 existing in later renders but not in the MWL file —
crosswalk Exceptions X-1/X-2). Against the live board, 224 is a
floor whose delta only a fresh render can state. That is a
staleness fact about the register's render, not an accounting
defect in the 224 — the accounting, on the documents, now closes.

Also untouched by this supplement, by design: the 35-vs-39 COMPLETE
question (X-1 — the +4 rows are not nameable from these documents)
