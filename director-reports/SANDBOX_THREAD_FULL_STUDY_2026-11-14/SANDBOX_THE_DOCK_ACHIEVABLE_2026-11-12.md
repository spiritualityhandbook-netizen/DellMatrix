## THE CONSTITUTION (Ace's rules, restated as law)

1. **ANALYZE FREELY.** A docked AI may analyze anything in
   the system — ideas, data, structure, history — EXCEPT
   sanitized classes (below).
2. **SANITIZED MEANS UNSEEN.** Data classified SANITIZED is
   not analyzed, not summarized, not inferred around. The
   fence is at perception, not at publication: the AI never
   holds it, so it can never leak it. (41[Sanitize] is the
   organ; WARD holds the fence.)
3. **READ THE MATRIX, WRITE THE SANDBOX.** The AI may use
   every tool in the Matrix whose effect is reading,
   analyzing, computing, or drafting. It may CHANGE data
   freely only inside its own sandboxes and rooms —
   temporary workspaces whose contents affect the Matrix
   not at all, beyond knowledge and record (what the AI
   learned there may be reported; the sandbox itself never
   writes through).
4. **CHANGE ONLY THROUGH PROPOSALS.** Any tool-use that
   would change ideas or data IN the Matrix is locked
   behind the proposal gate: the AI drafts a proposal (in
   its sandbox, with its evidence), the user reviews and
   accepts, and ONLY THEN do the change-capable tools unlock
   — for that proposal's scope, for that execution, receipted
   end to end. An accepted proposal lands in the Matrix as a
   round mutation (the Network's rounds law: change on
   paper, reconstructible, reversible by arithmetic).
5. **THE USER PROMPTS THE DOCK.** The user talks to the
   docked AI directly; prompting is conversation, not
   authorization — authorization is the accepted proposal,
   and nothing else is.
6. **ALL OTHER TOOLS ARE OPEN.** Outside the change-lock,
   the AI uses whatever tools it needs — the lock is
   surgical (change-in-Matrix), not a general leash.

## SECTIONED DOCKING

A dock is SECTIONED: an AI can be docked into the whole
core or into a named section (one goal, one organ's domain,
the Nursery alone). Sectioning sets three borders at once:
what it can analyze (its section + the shared commons),
which sandbox rooms it owns, and which proposals it may
raise (proposals about its section). Sections are SKIN and
FRAME applied to a guest: the borders are anatomy, not
preferences. A sectioned AI asking about another section
gets the honest refusal the system gives anyone.

## THE TWELVE DOCKS

Capacity as Ace states it: **up to 12 AIs docked at once.**
Each dock is a seat with four properties: IDENTITY (which
AI, named — Psalms in the Psalm Matrix's terms), SECTION
(where it is docked), LOADOUT (which skills are on for it),
and LEDGER (its own receipt stream — every analysis,
sandbox act, proposal, and execution under its name).
Twelve seats is a resource like any other: the Demand Beat
allocates attention among docked AIs the way it allocates
systems — needed Psalms foreground, the rest beating in
the background (present, sensing, not spending).

## SKILLS: SWITCHES AND CLASH THRESHOLDS

Every skill (ability) in the system carries, per Ace's
brief:

- **A SWITCH, twice over:** globally (the skill exists in
  the Matrix at all) and per-dock (this AI's loadout). Any
  skill can be turned off or on at either level, at any
  time; turning off is a beat-level act, receipted.
- **A CLASH CLASS**, set per skill from how it behaves
  under simultaneous use:
  - **EXCLUSIVE (threshold 1):** the skill breaks if two
    AIs hold it at once — one holder at a time. (DellMatrix
    precedent: state-sharing work runs SEQUENTIALLY — a
    threshold-1 rule Ace already lives by.)
  - **THRESHOLDED (threshold N):** fine at 2, fine at 3,
    breaks at N+1 — each such skill carries its OWN number
    (3 for one, 4 for another), measured, not guessed: the
    number is set from observed clash behavior and revised
    by evidence, like any other band.
  - **SHARED (no threshold):** any number of AIs may hold
    it simultaneously.
- **LEASES enforce the thresholds:** holding a clash-class
  skill is a lease — claimed when free capacity exists,
  queued when the count is at the number, stamped with its
  beat, expired with the round (no squatting: a lease not
  exercised decays, 16[Decay]'s duty). The lease ledger is
  part of the Network ledger: who held what, when, and what
  it did with it.

## HOW THE SEASON'S LEARNING IS BUILT IN

- **Organs:** the dock IS an organ system per guest — FEED
  (its intake/prompts), SENSE (its analysis), WARD (the
  sanitize fence + proposal gate), RETURN (its sandbox
  findings flow back as knowledge), SEED VAULT (its
  assignment pattern). A docked AI is a temporary citizen
  of the body, with a citizen's borders.
- **Demand Beat:** docked AIs and their skills rise and
  rest by the same selection physiology as systems —
  Filter (eligible) before Select (needed), always.
- **DuoBeta's laws:** the AI's sandbox learning is
  OBSERVATION; its proposals are PROPOSALS; nothing it
  concludes becomes Matrix truth by its say-so — the same
  gate, wearing a dock.
- **Rounds & receipts:** accepted-proposal executions land
  as round mutations, so an AI's every change to the Matrix
  is reconstructible and reversible.
- **The gorilla discipline:** a docked AI's analysis sweeps
  run under task sets with the GORILLA PAUSE — and its
  unaimed finds land as labeled surprises, not silent skips.
- **Sanitize before sight** is the Perception doctrine's
  dark twin: some things are not "unaimed at" — they are
  behind the fence, and the fence is physical.

## WHAT THE DOCK NEVER GETS
Repository access under its own hand (the Gemini fence,

### SEAT CONTROL

A seat is a place at a page where one AI docks. Rules:

- **One AI per seat, ever, at a time.** No two AIs share a
  seat simultaneously — a seat is a body-place, not a bench.
- **A page may hold many seats.** Ais dock TOGETHER at a
  page (the Psalm Matrix is the type case) — co-present,
  separately seated, each with its own ledger.
- **A docked AI controls its available seats:** it may OPEN
  or CLOSE access to any and all seats available to it, sit
  in ONE, in ALL, or in any combination in between, and
  work across its occupied seats in parallel. Seat occupancy
  is receipted (the Seat Control seed below), and closing a
  seat releases its skill leases with it.

```
24[Unlock]:: open the seats it may use
51[Select]:: sit in one, some, or all
62[Parallel]:: work across them at once
23[Lock]:: close what it leaves
90[Trace]:: seats held, receipted
```
(5 atoms, verified this sitting.)

### MEMORY ACROSS SEATS — INCLUDING THE RIGHT TO FORGET

A docked AI's memory is ONE mind across its seats: what it
learns in one seat, it knows in all of them — UNLESS it
chooses otherwise:

- **REMEMBER across seats** — the default: one continuous
  mind, seated in many places.
- **FORGET, on purpose** — it may forget: deliberately drop
  information from its live mind.
- **REMEMBER LATER** — forgetting here is SEALING, not
  shredding. Forgotten material goes to the sealed store
  (27[Checkpoint]) and can be restored (28[Rollback]) when
  the AI — or the user — calls it back. The two-store heart
  is the memory's shape: live mind / sealed mind.
- Every forget and every restore is receipted: the AI must
  be able to answer "what did you forget, when, and did you
  bring it back?" Forgetting is an instrument, never an
  accident and never a hiding place (sealed ≠ sanitized:
  the VAULT's fence is the system's; a seat's seal is the
  AI's own working tool, visible in its ledger).

### FORGET-AND-COMPARE (the fresh-eyes instrument)

The purpose of reversible forgetting, made a procedure:

```
27[Checkpoint]:: seal the memory away
16[Decay]:: forget it from the live mind
31[Simulate]:: run the subject fresh
28[Rollback]:: bring the sealed memory back
89[Diff]:: compare the two runs exactly
57[Compare]:: judge what memory added — and what it cost
```
(6 atoms, verified this sitting.)

Run a subject knowing everything → seal → forget → run the
SAME subject with a clean mind → restore → diff the two
runs. What changed is exactly what memory contributes —
its help AND its bias, measured on one receipt. This
instrument already exists in DellMatrix's engineering law
under another name: AGENTS.md §30's clean-copy differential
(a contract whose truth changes between polluted and clean
environments hides a dependency) IS forget-and-compare,
pointed at tests. Ace's brief generalizes it to a docked
AI's everyday instrument, and adds its twin:

- **PERSPECTIVE ABLATION:** take ONE piece of information
  (or one perspective) away, re-run, compare. Repeated
  ablations map which inputs actually move the result —
  sensitivity, measured instead of guessed.
- **MULTI-MIND PANELS:** the same procedures run with TWO,
  THREE, or more docked AIs — each seated separately at the
  same page, each producing its run (with or without its
  memory sealed), all runs compared on one diff. Agreement
  across independent minds is evidence; disagreement is a
  map of where the subject is genuinely undecided. (The
  pipeline already works this way at the authority level —
  Director and Keeper as two lanes over one record; the
  Dock brings the pattern down to working AIs.)

### TWO TOOL LAYERS

An AI arrives with tools of its OWN (its native abilities)
and may also lease the MATRIX's skills. The layers are
governed separately:

- **Personal tools** travel with the AI across seats and
  sections; they are part of its identity at the dock.
- **Matrix skills** are leased under the clash economy
  (Wave 1): thresholds, queues, expiries.
- **The only cross-rule is CLASH:** a personal tool and a
  leased skill (or two personal tools across docked AIs)
  that clash fall under the same threshold law — the system
  counts HOLDERS, not owners, when a clash is counted.

### TALKING TO THE HOUSE

- **The user chats with THE SYSTEM first.** The primary
  conversation is with DellMatrix itself — the body, not
  any guest. The system routes, selects, and answers as one.
- **Any docked AI can be ADDRESSED INDIVIDUALLY** — by name,
  in its seat — and speaks then in its own voice and ledger.
- **PERSONAS are assignable:** the user may give a docked AI
  a persona (02[Persona] — identity/role is a first-class
  Dell), and the persona rides that seat until changed:
  same AI, different seat, different persona is lawful —
  the ledger records which persona produced which work.

### The one-sentence relationship

**The Network is the body; the Dock is how guests live in
it.** A docked AI is a temporary citizen-organ: it senses
for the body (analysis), works in the body's gut-pockets
(sandboxes), eats only through the one wall (proposals),
never crosses the brain barrier (sanitized), sleeps and
forgets on the body's own heart machinery, holds skills the
way pathways hold capacity, and rises or rests by the same
Demand Beat that raises the body's own systems — so the
user can talk to THE SYSTEM as one creature, because with
the Network underneath, there IS one creature to talk to.

### What each gives the other
- Network → Dock: geography (zones), borders (WARD/SKIN),
  scheduling (Demand Beat, leases), memory machinery
  (two-store heart), safe change (rounds), one ledger.
- Dock → Network: WORKFORCE and WITNESS. Docked AIs are how
  the body gets more sensing, more drafting, and independent
  minds for its panels — the organs' work, multiplied by
  guests who can be seated, sectioned, sealed, compared,
  and dismissed, all without ever holding the body's keys.
- The future fusion (Ace's trajectory): when the Network
  fuses into DuoBeta, the Dock becomes the learning body's
  front door — other minds plug into a body that learns,
  and everything they do there is observation until the
  body's own gates say otherwise.

## WAVE 3 — CAPACITY SHAPE, COPY-IN WORK, TRAVEL (Ace, 2026-11-12)

### THE CAPACITY SHAPE

12 docked AIs is the CEILING, not the norm. The design
bands: **1 docked is the normal state** (most common),
**2–3 is the working panel range**, 4–12 is surge (big
panels, rare sittings). Physiology follows the bands: the
Demand Beat, lease thresholds, and ledger weight are sized
so ONE guest is nearly free, three is a conversation, and
twelve is a deliberate event — never an accident of drift.

### COPY-IN / WORK / PASTE-OR-SCRAP (the sandbox workflow)

The docked AI's central work pattern, Ace-specified:

```
84[Copy]:: take a copy, the original untouched
36[Inject]:: load it into the sandbox room
31[Simulate]:: work it to a result and an outcome
50[Manifest]:: present the whole result at the Nursery
60[Branch]:: accepted: it lands whole; denied: it is scrapped
90[Trace]:: copy, work, verdict — all receipted
```
(6 atoms, verified this sitting.)

- **COPY, never the original.** Anything in the Matrix can
  be copied into the AI's sandbox (84[Copy] — duplicate
  without consuming the original). The original is never
  the workbench; the Matrix cannot be scratched by work in
  progress, by construction.
- **The result is ONE unit.** The whole sandbox outcome —
  result plus its outcome record — is presented whole at
  the NURSERY (the proposal gate's venue, as standing
  doctrine already has it: proposals land in the Nursery;
  only the user's confirm moves them).
- **Accepted: it lands whole.** The entire result pastes
  onto the Matrix as one round mutation — not dribbled in
  piecemeal, not half-applied.
- **Denied: it is scrapped.** The copy and its work product
  are discarded (16[Decay]); the Matrix carries no residue
  of the denied attempt beyond the receipt that it was
  proposed and refused. Denial is clean, total, and cheap —
  which is what makes proposing safe enough to do often.

### TRAVEL AND EXAMINATION (the field instruments)

A docked AI does not sit in its room waiting for deliveries.
It TRAVELS the Matrix:

```
85[Move]:: travel the Matrix
56[Get]:: hold the idea
35[Discover]:: look inside it
58[Match]:: its resonance against the field
71[Measure]:: gravity, distance, weight
57[Compare]:: from every perspective that serves
90[Trace]:: the examination, receipted
```
(7 atoms, verified this sitting.)

- **HOLD:** the AI can take hold of an idea — a working grip
  that lets it carry the idea (or its copy) across the
  Matrix and set it beside others in place, examining each
  in the light of the rest.
- **LOOK INSIDE:** an idea's contents are examinable —
  its parts, its nested ideas, its records.
- **THE FIELD READINGS:** connections (what links it, how),
  **resonance** (what it harmonizes with — the affinity the
  program already computes as a pre-filter), **gravity**
  (how strongly the field pulls it and it pulls others),
  **distance** (how far it sits from any other idea in the
  field's geometry), **weight** (74[Weight] — its assigned
  and earned influence). These are the Plane/ForceField's
  actual instruments, read — never adjusted — by the guest.
- **ALL PERSPECTIVES:** examination runs from every
  perspective that serves (structural, historical, field,
  organ-by-organ) — perspective is a dial on the instrument,
  and the examination receipt names the perspectives used.

### THE HOLISTIC DEFAULT

Analysis takes into account **everything accessible that is
practical — which is usually everything.** The only two
filters on the AI's consideration are the SANITIZE FENCE
(never a filter of judgment; a wall) and PRACTICALITY
(the Harmonization Law's own filter: all *practical* related
parts; knowing what not to use is half the intelligence).
Practicality is judged per task and receipted when it
excludes something material, so "we didn't consider X" is
always an answerable question, never a silent gap.

### WAVE 3 ADDENDUM — WHAT "SCRAPPED" MEANS (Ace's correction, 2026-11-12)

Wave 3's wording — scrapped, "no residue beyond the receipt"
— is refined by Ace and the refinement governs: **the work
product is scrapped; the DENIAL is recorded, permanently.**

**The denial record** (one per denial, in the denial ledger):
- WHAT was proposed (the whole result, identified);
- WHO proposed it (which docked AI, which seat, which persona);
- WHO denied it, and WHEN;
- WHY — if specified (a stated reason is recorded verbatim);
- **A COMMENT — optional, with every denial.** The denier
  MAY leave a comment; its absence is lawful and itself
  unremarkable. When left, it rides the record forever.

**The denial stream is a learning stream.** Denials are
outcomes; outcomes are observations; observations, in
sufficient pattern, become proposals — through the same
Nursery gate, under the same standards as any proposal:

```
90[Trace]:: record what was denied
55[Set]:: why, if given; a comment, if left
38[Distill]:: watch the denial stream for patterns
73[Threshold]:: the pattern meets the standard
50[Manifest]:: propose — a what-not-to-do, or a new idea
92[Guard]:: the Nursery decides, as always
```
(6 atoms, verified this sitting.)

Two species of proposal grow from denial patterns:
- **WHAT-NOT-TO-DO proposals** — a recurring shape of denial
  (same approach, same flaw, same wall) proposes NEGATIVE
  guidance: a named anti-pattern the system — and future
  docked AIs — should stop proposing. Negative knowledge is
  knowledge; a map of walls is half a map of the room.
- **NEW-IDEA proposals** — a pattern of denials can reveal a
  shape the system keeps reaching for and the gate keeps
  refusing in its current form: the pattern itself proposes
  the corrected idea, if standards are met.

**Standards are the discipline:** one denial is an anecdote;
a pattern must meet the evidence standard (recurrence,
consistency, a statable rule) before it may propose — and
even then it PROPOSES. The Nursery decides, as always. A
denial without a comment still counts in the stream: the
gate's bare behavior, accumulated, is itself a signal no
comment is required to unlock. The scrap bin, in short, is
not a bin. It is the RETURN organ's richest intake: work
the Matrix refused, returning as the knowledge of what the
Matrix refuses — and, sometimes, what it is asking for.
