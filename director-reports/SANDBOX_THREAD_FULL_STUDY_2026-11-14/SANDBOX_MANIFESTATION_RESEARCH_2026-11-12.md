## 1. THE DIRECT CLAIM — THOUGHT BENDS EVENTS WITHOUT A CHANNEL

This is the claim "manifestation" usually means: intention reaches
out and rearranges events directly. It has been tested harder than
almost any claim in this whole research series. The record:

**PEAR (Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research, 1979–2007).**
Twenty-eight years of operators intending random event generators
to drift. Their own corpus: a departure of roughly **one bit in
ten thousand trials**, visible only in aggregate. The criticisms
are structural, not rhetorical (FACT): Alcock — poor controls and
documentation, data selection and optional stopping not ruled out;
Hansel — no satisfactory control series, no independent
replication; two German organizations failed to reproduce the
results, and PEAR at times failed to reproduce its own; and one
operator (a staff member) ran ~15% of the trials and accounted for
about half the total effect. The lab closed in 2007. **Grade:
CONTESTED at its strongest; never independently established; and
even if every number were taken at face value, the effect is
~0.01% — a magnitude that could not make a phone ring.**

**Precognition (Bem 2011, "Feeling the Future").** Nine
experiments, a flagship journal, enormous attention. Then the
replication record (FACT): Ritchie, Wiseman & French — three
preregistered replications, nothing. Galak et al. — seven
experiments, 3,289 participants, pooled effect ≈ 0.04, Bayes
factor ~70 in favor of the null. The proponent-run **Transparent
Psi Project (2023)**, designed with both camps: **49.89% correct
guesses against a 50% chance rate.** Large 2025 follow-ups: ~50%.
Proponents' meta-analyses (Bem et al. 2016, 90 experiments) report
a small pooled effect (g ≈ 0.09) — which critics attribute to
publication bias and study quality, and which preregistered work
does not reproduce. **Grade: DISPROVEN AS A RELIABLE ABILITY.** Its
true legacy is methodological: Bem's paper is a founding document
of the replication crisis and of preregistration itself.

**Presentiment** (bodies reacting seconds before a random stimulus;
Mossbridge et al. 2012 meta-analysis reports a small effect).
Contested on expectation-bias and analysis grounds; no accepted
mechanism; no preregistered consensus. **Grade: CONTESTED, weak.**

**Global Consciousness Project** (world events "cohering" a
worldwide RNG network). Criticized for post-hoc event selection —
the events and windows are chosen in ways that let noise pose as
signal. **Grade: UNSUPPORTED as evidence.**

**The quantum shortcut.** "Observation creates reality, therefore
thought creates reality." In physics, *observation* means
*interaction/measurement by anything* — a photon hitting a plate
observes. Decoherence does not require a mind, and no experiment
shows consciousness steering outcomes at human scales. **Grade:
UNSUPPORTED — a metaphor wearing a lab coat.**

**The Law of Attraction as sold** (visualize wealth; the universe
delivers). No controlled evidence for the direct claim — and one
directly contrary finding matters more: Oettingen's program shows
**pure positive fantasy reliably *reduces* achievement** (it
consumes the reward in imagination and lowers energy/effort),
while **mental contrasting** — wish, outcome, *obstacle*, plan
(WOOP) — improves it. **Grade: the popular form is worse than
useless; its honest neighbor works (§3).**

**Verdict on the direct claim:** after five decades and millions of
trials, there is **no demonstrated channel by which a thought, by
itself, moves an event it has no physical path to.** Any Mandell
instrument claiming one would be claiming past the entire record.
The sandbox keeps the door labeled, not locked — §4's ledger is
the instrument that could, in principle, produce personal evidence
worth reopening it. But the burden is the ledger's.

## 2. "I THOUGHT OF THEM — AND THEY CALLED" — FULLY EXPLAINED, NO REMAINDER NEEDED

The flagship experience is real as an *experience*. Its causes are
known, and there are five of them, usually stacked (all FACT as
mechanisms):

1. **The denominator you never see.** You think of people scores of
   times a week. The misses — thought of them, nothing happened —
   are not events, so they are not remembered. The one hit is
   unforgettable. Memory keeps the numerator and shreds the
   denominator, and the ratio feels miraculous. (Selective memory +
   confirmation bias; the Interpreter narrates the survivors.)
2. **The law of truly large numbers** (Diaconis & Mosteller 1989;
   Littlewood's "law of miracles"): a waking life samples roughly
   an event a second — over a month, a one-in-a-million coincidence
   is *expected*, about once a month, for everyone. Coincidences
   are not rare; *noticed* coincidences are just well-lit.
3. **Hidden cues (the subconscious workforce doing its job).** A
   song, a place, an anniversary, a photo glimpsed in a feed — the
   depths register the cue and surface the person as a "sudden
   thought." Often the *same cue reached them*: the shared song,
   the shared date, the mutual friend's post, the algorithm showing
   you both the same memory. Two minds primed by one environment
   "coincidentally" think of each other. No signal passed between
   the minds; a signal reached both from the world.
4. **The attention channel (from the Perception research).** Once
   thought of, the person is in your task set: you notice their
   name in the group chat you'd have scrolled past, you *answer*
   the call you'd have let ring, you count the near-misses as
   contact. The gorilla law, running in your favor.
5. **Your own behavior changed.** Thinking of them, you lingered on
   their profile, liked something, mentioned them to a mutual, were
   about to text. Modern "pop-ups" add a sixth, purely mechanical
   cause: platforms surface people your behavior has been orbiting.
   The phone is watching the orbit, not the thought.

Jung named the *feeling* (synchronicity, 1952) and was honest that
he was naming meaning, not mechanism. The sandbox takes meaning
seriously and mechanism literally: the feeling is data about
*you*; it is not evidence about the phone network.

## 3. WHAT THOUGHT DEMONSTRABLY *DOES* TO REALITY — THE SIX CHANNELS

Thoughts are not rays. They are **control signals inside a body
that acts** — and through that body they move the world every day,
along exactly six demonstrated channels. This is the usable ore.

| # | Channel | Mechanism (evidence) | What it can honestly "manifest" |
|---|---------|----------------------|---------------------------------|
| 1 | **ATTENTION** | Task set governs noticing (gorilla research; Wiseman's luck work) | Opportunities, warnings, people — *seen* instead of missed |
| 2 | **ACTION** | If–then intentions, mental contrasting/WOOP (Oettingen; Gollwitzer), first reps | Behavior change → outcomes: the job applied for, the rep done, the call made |
| 3 | **SIGNAL** | Expression changes others' behavior: you reach out, ask, state the goal; behavioral confirmation (expectations elicit matching behavior) | The person *actually* pops up — because you (or your visible trace) reached them |
| 4 | **ENVIRONMENT** | Written goals alter rooms, feeds, routines; informed networks route chances to known seekers | Referrals, introductions, offers — reality rearranged *socially*, in the open |
| 5 | **STATE / EXPECTANCY** | Honest expectancy changes physiology and performance (open-label placebo; state regulation widens the aperture) | Calmer body, better performance, wider noticing — the felt "alignment" |
| 6 | **RECORD** | Ledgers convert anecdotes to base rates (Calibration Ledger; this whole project's law) | The *knowledge* of whether any of it is working — and protection against fooling yourself |

**The sandbox theorem (DERIVED):** *A seed acts on reality exactly
as far as it changes what its reader notices, does, signals,
arranges, expects, or records — and not one inch further.* That is
not a small claim. Run honestly, those six channels account for
nearly every "manifestation" success story ever told — including
the ones in the testimonials — because the storytellers were,
without naming it, working the channels: they clarified (attention),
acted daily (action), told people (signal), changed their rooms and
circles (environment), expected and persisted (state), and
remembered the wins (record, uncorrected). Mandell's contribution
is to run the channels **deliberately, in the open, with receipts.**

## 5. THE GRADUATION RULE (how anything leaves this sandbox)

An instrument graduates to the Field Guide only when its own
ledger shows a measured effect across a full season (the
Coincidence Ledger: ≥4 weeks; the Signal/Beacon: ≥1 month of
arrival counts against a silent baseline). A claim graduates
*never* — claims stay graded. And the flagship claim itself
graduates only via Seed S-1's own Branch: a sustained,
pre-logged beat of the base rate, replicated by a second logger.
Until then, the honest sentence stands: **thoughts act on reality
through the thinker — and the thinker, aimed well, is quite a lot
of reality to act through.**
