## 1. THE GREAT ADJUDICATION — COGITATE, 2025 (FACT about the test; the theories remain CONTESTED)

In 2025 the field did something DellMatrix would recognize
instantly: an **adversarial collaboration**. The two leading
scientific theories of consciousness agreed *in advance*, in
writing, on what evidence would count against their own theory:

- **Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT)** — Baars, Dehaene,
  Changeux: a thought becomes conscious when it wins a competition
  and is **broadcast** across the brain, with a late burst of
  prefrontal "ignition." Consciousness = global availability.
- **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)** — Tononi: consciousness
  *is* a specific mathematical property — information integrated
  beyond what the parts hold separately (Φ), concentrated in a
  **posterior "hot zone"** at the back of the brain, sustained for
  as long as the experience lasts.

Theory-neutral teams then ran the preregistered experiments:
**256 participants, multiple labs, three measurement technologies
(fMRI, MEG, intracranial EEG)**. Published in *Nature*, 30 April
2025, with *both camps as co-authors*.

**Result: neither theory's core prediction survived intact.**
IIT's required sustained posterior synchronization did not appear.
GNWT's ignition was weaker than predicted and absent at stimulus
offset where the theory required it. Content of experience tracked
most strongly with posterior sensory regions — a point for IIT's
geography, against its mechanism; prefrontal involvement existed
but did not behave as the broadcast story said. Both camps signed
the paper anyway.

DERIVED_FACT for this project: the Cogitate design *is* the Ace
standard running at civilizational scale — preregistered success
contracts, falsification agreed before data, adversaries sharing
one record, verdicts published without either side claiming a win
the evidence didn't give. Consciousness science, at its best, now
works like this pipeline works. And its verdict on the deepest
question is a clean, honest **UNKNOWN** — which per Ace's doctrine
is a location, not a failure.

(The aftershock is also on record: a 2023 open letter signed by
100+ researchers called IIT "pseudoscience" over its panpsychist
implications; Tononi's camp rebutted in detail. Status: the field's
loudest fight, unresolved; Φ remains practically uncomputable for
real brains, which is IIT's deepest practical wound.)

## 2. WHAT CONSCIOUSNESS IS *FOR* — THE BROADCAST AND THE AUDITOR (the sturdy part)

While the metaphysics fights, the *functional* picture is
comparatively solid. Dehaene, Lau & Kouider's 2017 *Science* review
splits "consciousness" into computations that can actually be
studied:

- **C0 — unconscious processing.** The workforce of round two:
  face recognition, sentence parsing, chess evaluation, evidence
  accumulation, reinforcement learning. Vast, parallel, fast — and
  the review's blunt sentence deserves quoting in spirit: most of
  our intelligence is down here.
- **C1 — global availability.** The small subset selected for
  broadcast becomes available to *everything at once*: memory,
  planning, language, deliberate action. This is what consciousness
  buys that C0 cannot do: hold one thing **stable over time**,
  combine information from unrelated domains, handle the genuinely
  **novel** (no chunk exists yet), and **report** — make the content
  available to other minds. Conscious processing is slow, serial,
  and tiny in capacity (a handful of items) — it is a stage, not a
  floodlight, and its smallness is the point: broadcast is expensive,
  so admission is competitive.
- **C2 — self-monitoring (metacognition).** The system modeling its
  own processing: knowing that you know, sensing an error before
  you can say what's wrong, confidence. C2 is fallible — confidence
  and accuracy dissociate routinely — and trainable: calibration
  improves when predictions are scored against outcomes.

Attention Schema Theory (Graziano; EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE, elegant,
unproven) adds the sharpest functional claim: to *control*
attention, the brain builds a simplified **model of its own
attention** — a cartoon, deliberately leaving out the machinery —
and subjective awareness is what that cartoon depicts. On this
account, "I am aware of the apple" is the brain reading its own
control diagram. Whether or not AST is true, it hands this project
a testable discipline: **your model of where your attention is, is
itself a representation — cartoon-grade — and can be checked
against where your attention observably went.** (See Cell 9.)

## 3. THE HALF-SECOND SCANDAL — AND ITS QUIET RETRACTION (FACT, revised)

Libet (1983): brain preparation for a voluntary finger movement
(the readiness potential, RP) begins ~550 ms before the movement;
reported conscious intention arrives only ~200 ms before. The
popular conclusion — "your brain decided before you did; free will
is an illusion" — conquered the culture.

The modern record is far less convenient for that headline:
- **Schurger, Sitt & Dehaene (2012, PNAS):** the RP's smooth ramp
  is what you mathematically *get* when you average backward from
  threshold crossings of ongoing neural noise — a leaky stochastic
  accumulator. The "decision signal" may be an artifact of the
  averaging itself.
- **Soon et al. (2008):** fMRI decoding "predicts" choices seconds
  early — at ~**60%** accuracy against a 50% coin flip. Real, and
  nearly useless as determinism.
- **Maoz et al. (2019):** RPs precede *arbitrary*, meaningless
  choices; for **deliberate, value-based decisions**, the classic
  RP is largely absent. The scandal was measured on the least
  will-like acts imaginable.
- **The veto is real and timed (Schultze-Kraft et al. 2016,
  PNAS):** in a real-time brain-computer-interface duel, people
  could abort an already-preparing movement up to ~**200 ms before
  execution** — Libet's own "free won't" — after which a true point
  of no return closes the gate.

DERIVED_FACT synthesis: for spontaneous micro-actions, initiation
is mostly the workforce's; consciousness's native powers are
**selection, veto, sustained direction, and design-in-advance**
(the if–then of round one is precisely consciousness legislating
for its future automatic self). Consciousness is less the author of
each act than the **editor-in-chief and legislature** of the whole
operation: it rarely writes the first draft of anything, and it
owns the publish button, the style guide, and the laws the drafts
must obey. (With the honest footnote that even veto signals show
pre-conscious precursors in some studies; the editor has staff too.)

## 4. MEASURING THE LIGHT (FACT, with limits)

The **Perturbational Complexity Index** (Casali et al. 2013,
Science Translational Medicine; benchmarked by Casarotto et al.
2016 in 150+ patients): zap the cortex with a magnetic pulse
(TMS), record the echo (EEG), and measure the echo's complexity.
Consciousness, operationally, is **integration + differentiation**:
many regions responding, each differently, in a pattern too rich
to be a mere echo and too organized to be noise. A cutoff near
**PCI 0.31** separated conscious from unconscious states across
wakefulness, REM sleep, deep sleep, three anesthetics, coma,
unresponsive wakefulness, minimally conscious state, and locked-in
syndrome — *independent of whether the person could move or
report anything*. It detected consciousness in people behavior
alone had written off.

Limits, on the record: PCI indexes a *capacity/level*, not
contents — it cannot say what someone experiences; and a 2025
deep-sedation study found complexity measures did not distinguish
awakenings with reported experience from those without. PCI is the
best consciousness meter in existence and it is still a proxy —
names are not evidence, and meters are not the thing metered.

DERIVED_FACT for DellMatrix: this is the Inspector's creed in
neurology form — when reports are impossible or untrustworthy,
**perturb the system and measure the complexity of its response.**
A system that answers a poke with one stereotyped flinch is in a
different state from one that answers with a rich, differentiated,
integrated response. Ace's whole testing philosophy — probe,
don't ask — has a clinical-grade cousin.

## 5. THE SETTLED SHAPE OF THE MYSTERY (honest inventory)

- FACT: conscious contents are a tiny, selected, broadcast subset
  of total processing; the selection is competitive; self-monitoring
  exists and errs; consciousness levels track brain complexity in
  ways now measurable at a bedside.
- FACT: reports of reasons are constructions (round two's
  Interpreter) — including reports about consciousness itself.
- CONTESTED: which mechanism makes processing conscious (broadcast,
  integration, higher-order representation, prediction) — Cogitate
  wounded the top two and crowned neither.
- UNKNOWN: why any of it is accompanied by experience at all — the
  hard problem (Chalmers) stands untouched by every result in this
  document. Every instrument here measures function, structure, and
  complexity. None measures the light being on. Anyone who tells
  you otherwise is selling something.

Performance script:
1. 97[Define] — "THE BROADCAST." Use when something matters and keeps failing to govern your behavior — it was felt, never staged.
2. 35[Discover] — Inventory the competition: what is loud right now (usually a feeling, a notification, a worry).
3. 51[Select] — Choose ONE content deliberately. Loudest ≠ selected. This choosing is the conscious act itself.
4. 09[Show] — Say it or write it in a full plain sentence. Vague contents can't be routed; sentences can.
5. 19[Drive] — Hold the sentence steady for a minute. Drift is normal; returning to the exact wording is the exercise.
6. 08[Create] — Route it: memory (have I met this before?), plan (what does my week say?), body (what does it signal?), a friend's eyes (what would they notice?).
7. 38[Distill] — One next action, distilled from all departments' replies.
8. 95[Commit] — Decide/commit during the broadcast. Once the stage clears, the workforce resumes on old instructions.
9. 10[Keep] — Write the sentence down. Broadcast + trace = the thought can now be re-staged anytime, by you or audited later.

## 7. ROUND THREE'S VERDICT (DERIVED_FACT)

- Consciousness, functionally, is the **broadcast stage plus the
  auditor's desk**: C1 makes a little information available to
  everything; C2 watches the watcher and can be calibrated. Its
  proven powers are selection, sustained direction, veto, novel
  combination, report, and legislation-in-advance. Its proven
  limits are capacity (a handful of items), speed (slow), and
  authorship (it rarely drafts first).
- The deepest open question in science — why experience exists at
  all — remains honestly UNKNOWN after the largest, fairest test
  ever run. Round three's respect for that unknown is not a gap in
  this research; it is this research's standard, applied to the
  hardest case.
- For DellMatrix, the convergence is now three rounds deep: the
  Interpreter problem (round two) said narratives can't be trusted
  without traces; Cogitate (round three) says even our best
  theories of the narrator must preregister and submit; PCI says
  when you cannot trust reports, perturb and measure the response.
  **Mandell's founding instincts — visible chains, checkable steps,
  evidence over story — are what consciousness science itself is
  converging toward, from inside its own crisis.**
