### Network laws (the organ system's constitution, draft)

1. **No flow without a receipt.** Every hand-off between
   organs is a ledger line. An unreceipted flow is a leak,
   and leaks are defects by definition.
2. **Organs report; only the heartbeat routes.** No organ
   commands another organ directly (the body agrees: a
   muscle doesn't order the liver).
3. **Two signal speeds, kept separate.** SENSE's fast wired
   loop for safety and motion; TUNE's slow broadcasts for
   retuning (warm the battery before dawn, cool the cabin
   before arrival). Slow may never drive a fast act.
4. **WARD can isolate any organ, including the heart's
   stores** — a failing organ is quarantined, and the body
   drives on degraded, receipted, honest (limp-home is a
   state, not a shame).
5. **The count is life.** Charge, temperature, and pressure
   held in bands by small constant corrections — the
   network's homeostasis — and Profile (49) reads the whole
   body's waste-per-mile as its one health number.
6. **The Seed Vault is read-only on the road.** The pattern
   that makes the next car never edits itself while driving.
