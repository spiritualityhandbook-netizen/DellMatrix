# §1 THE EFFICIENCY DOCTRINE — ONE IDEA, ONE ADDRESS, NO DOUBLES

Ace's concern, stated plainly: a single idea accumulates applications,
readings, and history everywhere it travels — its data could grow
without bound. The Grand Plan's answer: **the growth is an illusion
created by counting readings as data. The program already owns every
mechanism needed to keep one idea's true footprint small; the doctrine
names them, binds them into one law, and adds the one instrument that
proves it continuously.**

**The footprint law.** For any idea:
FOOTPRINT = ADDRESS (constant) + BODY (its content, stored ONCE)
+ RECEIPTS (compact ledger entries: ids, hashes, verdicts)
+ HISTORY (shared, not per-idea — see 3 below).

**QUALIFICATION (SD-GP-01, 2026-11-14):** this doctrine states
REQUIREMENTS, not established results. Permanent keys, shared
generations, and derived views do not by themselves prove a
constant or bounded total footprint: retention growth,
checkpoint accumulation, cache invalidation, and reconstruction
(derive-on-read) cost are UNMEASURED. The footprint receipt
(§1.6) is the instrument that must produce those measurements;
until it does, every efficiency claim in this section is a
target the design must meet, not a fact the design has met.

1. **PERMANENT ADDRESS, SOFT PAYLOAD** (KeyLedger; Existence truth:
   "Keys permanent. Units may move or soft-forget payload; address
   remains."). An idea's identity costs one key forever. Content may
   be soft-forgotten radially; the address never grows, never dies.
2. **STORE ONCE, DERIVE EVERYTHING ELSE.** The lattice views, the
   notes and the Tree chord, the harmony score, Gravity's wells,
   Growth's plants, the weather line, every behavior map — all are
   REBUILT from source records on demand, never stored as copies.
   The law in one line: **a derived thing stored is a future
   contradiction (it can drift from its source) AND wasted space —
   derivation is both the honesty rule and the efficiency rule.**
   This discipline already exists piecemeal (the lattice's
   rebuild-only contract); the Grand Plan makes it the default for
   every reading in §2's Senses house.
3. **HISTORY IS FIELD-LEVEL, NOT PER-IDEA.** Checkpoints seal the
   whole state as hash-chained generations. An idea does not carry
   its own history copies; its past lives in the sealed generations
   it participated in, shared across the entire field. History cost
   is therefore amortized over everything, and grows with the number
   of ERAS, not the number of ideas × their travels. Rollback
   converges by copying a sealed generation into live state (the
   standing contract) — the past is visited, never duplicated into
   the present.
4. **ESSENCE + REFERENCE INSTEAD OF COPIES** (the program's own
   compression doctrine, tour DD-20/DD-28): **Distill** = the
   self-contained essence (reduced, usable standalone); **Compress**
   = a reference tag that points home (the Zipper: Expand follows
   the reference and restores the full original). Moving an idea
   moves its reference or its essence — the full body travels only
   when the work truly needs the body. The Macro shelf holds copies
   deliberately, bounded, leaving only by Ace's hand.
5. **LEDGERS RECORD RECEIPTS, NOT TRANSCRIPTS.** Outcome, circuit,
   duo, and lineage ledgers append compact entries. Where a log
   today silently caps (resonance log, 80), the Elastic Limits
   doctrine replaces silence with a stated, receipted, demand-grown
   ring. Truncation is never efficiency — the census's 240-char
   Nursery-store cut is the standing counterexample: **efficiency
   must be declared (bounds labeled, scopes named), never smuggled.**
6. **THE INSTRUMENT: THE FOOTPRINT RECEIPT.** Measure (tour DD-46:
   measure ANY property) + the Weight sweep + Profile make any
   idea's total cost a reportable number: body bytes + ledger
   entries naming it + generations it appears in. In the Grand
   System this receipt is a standard reading in the census: an idea
   whose footprint grows without a stated reason is a named defect
   (a LEAK), filed like any other. Efficiency stops being a hope
   and becomes a measurement with an owner.

What this doctrine REFUSES: per-idea history copies; stored
projections; silent truncation; second ledgers for the same truth.
What it COSTS: compute at read time (derivation) — paid once per
reading, cached by permanent address where a cache is lawful
(Seed Vault pattern: compute once at deep budget, serve forever).
