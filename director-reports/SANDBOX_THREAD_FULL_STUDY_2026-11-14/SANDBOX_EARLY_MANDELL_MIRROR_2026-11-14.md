## 2. THE TRANSLATION BENCH (today's parser vs early text)

Fed to parse_seed at current main:

- "08[Create_PreForm_GodWorkspace_Mandale] >>> 14[Bind] >>>
  09[Show]" — PARSES. Atoms resolve BY NUMBER (8, 14, 9);
  the long early names ride as the atom's term. Early seeds
  are structurally readable today.
- "10[Keep_NovaTruth_Crystalline_Locked] >>> 23[Lock]" —
  PARSES (10, 23).
- "01[Lexicon_Engine] >>> 00[Nova] >>> 04[Transformix]" —
  PARSES (1, 0, 4) — even coined names ride through on their
  numbers.
- "12[Test_Clash+Resolve_x40] >>> 04[Apply_Confirmed_Only]"
  — ZERO ATOMS. The "+" packed into a name breaks today's
  atom pattern: the early habit of fusing two ideas into one
  bracket ("Clash+Resolve") does not survive the grammar.
- "26:Temp[H]" — ZERO ATOMS. The colon-manor notation
  (NN:Name[...]) is dead; today it is written 26[Temp].

Execution narrows what notation allows: flow_executor
classifies ">" (FlowTo) and ">>" (FlowThru) as executable;
">>>" REQUIRES_RUNTIME_SUPPORT (refused at execution); ":",
"::", ":>", "<:" DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY. VB.3's sixteen flows
shrank to a grammar of nine (<<[Delta], <:>, >>>, >>, :>,
<:, ::, >, :) of which two run. The +Flow / =Flow / /\Flow /
#Flow / ^Flow / !Flow / ?Flow / {}Flow family is absent from
today's grammar entirely. <<[Delta] (DeltaFlow, the
anti-flow pull from a checkpoint) remains in the grammar,
and its JOB is done today by 27[Checkpoint] / 28[Rollback]
and the checkpoint/sealed-generation machinery.
