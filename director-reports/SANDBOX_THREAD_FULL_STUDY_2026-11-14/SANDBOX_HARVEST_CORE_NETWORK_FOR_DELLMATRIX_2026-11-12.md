# SANDBOX — THE HARVEST CORE NETWORK FOR DELLMATRIX

Status: **SANDBOX** (the Harvest Engine sandbox family,
2026-11-12). Ace's correction of aim, same sitting: the body
and the car were the TEMPLATES. The Harvest Core Network is
built **for DellMatrix itself** — a general organ system that
can be wrapped around **any idea**, so that any idea in the
Matrix can feed, circulate, recover, guard, prune, and
reproduce the way a body does. The car was the first host.
It is not the point.

## THE CLAIM, STATED PLAINLY

An idea in DellMatrix is a living system with flows: things
come in (attention, evidence, material, work), things are
spent (execution, decisions, outputs), and things are thrown
away (failed attempts, stale branches, spent context,
unrecovered results). Today's ideas mostly run like today's
cars: one stream in, a dozen leaks out, no ledger. The
Harvest Core Network gives any host idea a BODY: twelve
organs, one bus, one heartbeat, one ledger — so the idea
wastes as little of what flows through it as its nature
allows, and can always show its books.

**THE TWO-STORE HEART** ( inside the BUS): every idea
already beats with two stores in DellMatrix — **live working
state** (mutable, fast, the blood in motion) and **sealed
generations** (checkpoints: immutable, the body's memory of
its own past shapes). The mirror names what exists: an idea
whose two stores disagree is an idea with a heart condition,
and rollback convergence is its medicine.

**THE LEDGER** is not an organ; it is the blood's property:
every flow between organs is a receipt (90[Trace]). An
unreceipted flow is a leak; leaks are defects by definition.

## THE NETWORK FOR ANY HOST — THE HEARTBEAT (general form)

Verified in the car sitting; it was already host-general:

```
54[Query]:: every organ reports its state
57[Compare]:: actual against intended
60[Branch]:: route each surplus and each shortage
25[Pulse]:: broadcast the corrections
62[Parallel]:: organs act together, none waits
21[Merge]:: all flows meet on the bus
90[Trace]:: the network ledger closes the beat
```

For an idea, one beat reads: every organ reports the idea's
state → the network compares it to the idea's intent →
surpluses (spare attention, reusable results) and shortages
(missing evidence, stale parts) are routed → corrections
pulse out → all organs act at once → flows meet on the bus →
the ledger closes the beat. The idea's **homeostasis bands**
are its health numbers (evidence freshness, open-question
count, stale-part ratio, budget burn) held in range by these
small constant corrections.

## PROOF OF GENERALITY — ONE NON-CAR HOST, SKETCHED

**Host idea: a research question** (say, a DellMatrix
investigation). FEED: papers and probes enter as proposals
only. SENSE: the question's state (what's known, what's
UNKNOWN) swept per beat. MOVE: experiments spend the budget.
RETURN: failed experiments flow back as *named dead ends* —
the season's own habit (the Voynich workbench ran exactly
this way: every falsified hypothesis stayed on the books and
fed the next phase). WARD: the honesty lock — no claim
crosses into "finding" without its evidence. CLEAR: dead
leads fade on schedule instead of haunting the context.
TUNE: DuoBeta-style reranking of which lead looks fertile.
SEED VAULT: the question's seed — the chain that, run again,
regrows the whole investigation. The car never appears; the
body works.

## CONSTITUTION (carried from the car sitting, generalized)

1. No flow without a receipt.
2. Organs report; only the heartbeat routes.
3. Two signal speeds, kept separate — fast SENSE loop,
   slow TUNE broadcasts; slow may never drive a fast act.
4. WARD can isolate any organ; a degraded host runs
   degraded, receipted, honest.
5. The count is life: bands held by small corrections;
   Profile reads the host's waste ratio as its health number.
6. The Seed Vault is read-only while the host runs.

This is a design synthesis in a sandbox: it names and wires

## THE NORTH STAR — ONE BODY, DEMAND-SELECTED (Ace, 2026-11-12)

> Take everything that can be within the Network and apply

> background operation: any and all systems within are the

### Translation into DellMatrix's standing vocabulary

The vision is the eligibility stack given a body and a
heartbeat. DellMatrix already distinguishes STORED >
ELIGIBLE > SELECTED > ROUTABLE > USED. The Network adds
the physiology that moves a system between those states
per beat:

- **ALL systems are within the idea** — the body is whole:
  every organ present, alive, beating quietly. Presence is
  ELIGIBILITY, not activity.
- **BACKGROUND operation = the autonomic mode.** Most
  organs, most of the time, run autonomically — sensing,
  clearing, returning, holding bands — receipted, unattended,
  exactly as the body's gut and glands work without asking
  the mind. Background is not OFF and not IDLE; it is the
  body keeping itself alive.
- **SELECTED operation = the attended mode.** When a subject
  arrives, the systems that subject requires rise to the
  foreground — selected, routed, used — while the rest stay
  autonomic. This is Ace's growth law (growth only with
  SELECT) generalized from growth to ALL operation, and it
  is LM-7's Harmonization Law with hands: consider all
  practical related parts; knowing what NOT to raise is half
  the intelligence.
- **The missing piece Ace names is the DEMAND CHECK:** a
  standing organ-duty — per beat, read the user's current
  state and the moment's demand, and let THAT decide the
  selection. Not the loudest system, not the most recent
  system: the required ones, for this subject, now.

### THE DEMAND BEAT (verified this sitting, 7 atoms)

```
54[Query]:: read the user's state and the moment's demand
35[Discover]:: which systems this subject needs
52[Filter]:: only the eligible may rise
51[Select]:: foreground the needed systems
62[Parallel]:: the rest keep beating in the background
59[Route]:: assemble them on the subject
90[Trace]:: what rose, what rested, and why — receipted
```

Gloss: ask what the user needs now → find which systems
this subject requires → the ineligible may not rise no
matter how loud → raise the needed → everything else keeps
its quiet autonomic beat → the raised systems assemble ON
the subject (not near it) → the selection itself is
receipted, so any beat can answer "why these systems, why
now."

### Guardrails that keep the North Star honest
- Selection never bypasses hard law: WARD and eligibility
  precede raising (Filter sits before Select in the seed —
  that order is load-bearing).
- Background organs keep their receipts; autonomic never
  means unaccountable.
- The demand check reads the user's state AS PRESENTED in
  the work (current task, current subject, current moment)
  — it is a routing sense, never surveillance; its border
  is the SKIN organ's border.
- One body, one authority: the Network connects parts; it
  does not govern them. Ace, the Director, and the Keeper
  remain the seats; the Network is the body their decisions
  move through.
