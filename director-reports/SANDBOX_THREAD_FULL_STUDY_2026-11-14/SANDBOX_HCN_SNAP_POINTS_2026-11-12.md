# SANDBOX STUDY — SNAPPING THE HARVEST CORE NETWORK ELSEWHERE

Status: **SANDBOX STUDY** (Ace's question, 2026-11-12).
The Network was designed host-general and first fused, on
paper, with DuoBeta. Ace asks: where ELSE in DellMatrix
could it snap? Method: for each candidate host — what the
snap gains, what it risks, and a verdict class:
NATURAL (the host already half-is a body), DESCRIPTIVE
(the snap only renames what exists — useful lens, no new
capability, must not pretend otherwise), RISKY (authority
or duplication hazards), COSTUME (fails the organ anatomy:
the "organs" would be names without jobs).

Governing cautions (from AGENTS.md, binding even in a
sandbox): never create a second authority where a canonical
one exists; capability movement outranks process movement;
a snap that changes no capability is a lens, and must be
labeled a lens.

## THE SNAP MAP

**1. The Nursery — NATURAL.**
The Nursery already has FEED (proposals arrive), CLEAR (the
3/7/14/30 fade law), a border (only Ace's confirm promotes).
Snapping the Network gives the proposal population a body:
SENSE sweeps proposal health (aging, orphans), RETURN
recycles the substance of faded proposals back into the
inflow as raw material (today a faded proposal's content
dies with it), TUNE ranks by the standing formula, WARD
guards the one-door rule (nothing enters except as a
proposal — Gemini's outputs included). GAIN: rejected and
faded work stops evaporating. RISK: low — jurisdictions
already exist.

**2. Every idea on the Plane — NATURAL (the original pitch).**
The general Network was designed for this: each Plane idea
a body with anchor, ring, flows, vault. GAIN: the whole
field becomes accountable bodies instead of loose nodes.
RISK: bookkeeping weight per idea; a throttled form (organs
wake only for SELECTED ideas — Ace's growth law) is the
honest version.

**3. A docked Psalm (Gemini) — NATURAL, and quietly large.**
A Psalm is a worker with a doctrine: commands flow
Ace > Director > Uni through program surfaces; output is
always PENDING; no repository access. Wrap the Psalm in the
Network and the doctrine becomes anatomy: FEED = its task
intake (one door), WARD = the command borders, RETURN = its
outputs recycled as proposals, SENSE = its own usage and
drift watched, SEED VAULT = its assignment pattern. GAIN:
any future docked AI inherits a body with the safety laws
as organs instead of as memos. RISK: low-medium — the snap
must never grant the Psalm organs that act (its MOVE stays
propose-only).

**4. A Goal (the Goals tab) — NATURAL.**
A goal is an idea with a lifetime: it feeds (tasks,
evidence), spends (work), and today its waste (dead ends,
superseded plans) mostly evaporates between sessions. The
Network gives a goal a ledger of its own, rounds with
mutation receipts (the goal's plan changes ON PAPER), empty
chairs for its open questions, and a seed in the vault —
which is exactly what a GOAL.md file is trying to be, with
the organs made explicit. GAIN: goal continuity stops
depending on session memory. RISK: low.

**5. The whole Program — DESCRIPTIVE-to-NATURAL, handle
with care.** The Program as one host: its organs are its
real subsystems (Nursery, Plane, DuoBeta, execution,
persistence). The snap mostly DESCRIBES the architecture in
body language — valuable as the one picture of the whole —
but it must not mint a program-level "heartbeat" that
routes over the Director's and Ace's authority. The
heartbeat of the whole program is the WORKFLOW, and the
workflow's seats are people and authorities, not organs.
Verdict: draw it, learn from it, do not let the drawing
govern.

**6. The workflow pipeline itself (Ace/Director/Keeper/
Uni/Gemini circuit) — DESCRIPTIVE, fenced.** It already
runs like a body: 15-minute and hourly beats, gates,
ledgers (the state file), quarantine for mismatched inputs.
Snapping the Network here renames seats as organs — and
that is exactly why it is fenced: seats hold AUTHORITY,
organs hold JOBS, and confusing the two is how a second
authority is born. Lens only, forever, unless Ace rules
otherwise.

**7. The knowledge stack (stored → eligible → selected →
routable → used → observed → learned) — NATURAL.**
Each knowledge item as a small body is overkill; the STACK
as one host is not: FEED = storage intake, WARD = the hard
eligibility laws (already the strictest borders in the
program), BUS = routing, CLEAR = the bounded-store counters
already dropping the oldest. GAIN: the influence pipeline
gains per-stage health (which stage is clogged, which is
starving) on one tally wheel. RISK: low.

**8. A single Dell — COSTUME (the instructive failure).**
A Dell is an atom: ADDRESS × OPERATION × PRODUCT × TEST ×
TRACE. Wrapping one Dell in twelve organs inverts the
anatomy (the organs would each be smaller than the thing
they serve, jobs without flows to carry). The snap fails
the costume test and thereby proves the Network's floor:
**the smallest honest host is a system with flows** — an
idea, a goal, a worker, a population — never a single atom.
Below that floor, use the Dell anatomy itself.

**9. The research workbench (any investigation) — NATURAL.**
Already proven in sketch (the Network file's generality
proof): the Voynich workbench RAN as this body informally
all day — FEED of probes, RETURN of falsified hypotheses as
named dead ends, WARD as the honesty lock, rounds as the
phases themselves. GAIN of the formal snap: the next
investigation inherits the body instead of reinventing it.

## THE FRACTAL POINT (the snap map's real discovery)

The snaps NEST. A goal (host) contains ideas (hosts), which
contain sub-ideas; the Program contains goals; a Psalm
serves inside several. If the Network snaps at more than
one level, DellMatrix becomes **bodies within bodies** —
and the ledgers nest with them: an idea's beat is a flow in
its goal's beat; a goal's round is a mutation in the
Program's ring. This is LM-7's fractal zoom wearing the
body: the same anatomy at every scale, receipts rolling
upward, bands within bands. It is also the discipline's
limit: snap at too many levels and the bookkeeping eats the
work (the tally must stay cheaper than the harvest). The
study's rule of thumb: **snap where flows are real and
losses hurt; skip levels where the body would only be a
drawing.**

## VERDICT TABLE
Natural: Nursery, Plane ideas (selected), Psalm, Goal,
knowledge stack, research workbench.
Descriptive/fenced: the whole Program (picture, not
governor), the workflow pipeline (lens only).
Costume: a single Dell (below the floor).
Planned (Ace's trajectory): DuoBeta.
