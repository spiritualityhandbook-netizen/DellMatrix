## §1 THE DOCTRINE, IN ACE'S WORDS AND IN MECHANISM WORDS

Ace: Smith is **the other side of Verita**.

Mechanism words: there is one question — **HOW DO TWO THINGS MEET?**
— and it has two independent sides.

- **VERITA — the CONTENT side.** Judged from inside the things
  themselves: does this idea hold together (solo: clarity, density,
  self-agreement, non-fog, goal load), and do these two agree
  (pair: coherence over the canonical vesica + token Jaccard, with
  residue marks where they don't)? Verita never asks what happened
  when the two were actually brought together. It grades the
  *contents*, before and regardless of any meeting.
- **SMITH — the FLOW side.** Judged at the boundary, in the event:
  when something arrives at something else, how much of it gets in
  and how much bounces back? Era 1's literal form: the reflection
  coefficient Γ = (ZL − Z0)/(ZL + Z0) — center of the disk = perfect
  match (everything passes), edge = total reflection (nothing
  passes), and the standing wave between = the residue pattern a
  partial meeting leaves standing. Smith never asks whether the two
  *contents* agree. It grades the *meeting*.

Neither side implies the other. That independence is the whole
point of having two sides — and it is testable in principle (§3).
