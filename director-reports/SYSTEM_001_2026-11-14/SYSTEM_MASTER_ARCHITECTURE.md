## The Tree of Harmonic Life — five layers, five contracts

Per the Director's §4 separation (register SYS-C17..C21 family):
**L1 exact kernel** (FCC coordinates/adjacency/shells/slices —
mathematics verified by THL-SM-01; admission pending Keeper gate +
Director ruling) · **L2 occupancy** (populated/empty/reserved —
spatial facts only) · **L3 semantic mapping** (idea ↔ location, with
provenance, reversible; **blocked on Q-F1 idea radius and placement
admission**) · **L4 derived mathematics** (overlap, topology, voids,
harmonic readings — each with validity status; no single "truth
score") · **L5 presentation** (Tree/Flower/cube/text readings of the
same data — witnesses, never authorities).

Two standing rules ride on every Tree contract: (a) product Flower
comparisons are made against the **defined (111) slice**, never an
unrestricted projection (THL-SM-01's cross-check was slice-correct
and is the fixture's pinned form); (b) proximity never implies
semantic connection — branches derive from an explicit adjacency or
routing rule only.

## Ace's feature, registered: the Dell Lattice Index (FI-001)

Ace (2026-11-14): *"Dells as numbers — since they are numbers, can
we put them in a lattice in a way to use them faster and pull them
faster?"* **Answer: yes — as a derived index, under the placement
law.** Design sketch (full entry in `SYSTEM_DECISION_REGISTER.md`
§Feature Intake): every Dell already is an address (Foundation
factor 1). The index places the 99 addresses in a lattice
(HarmonicLattice is the candidate substrate) so retrieval is spatial
— direct coordinate resolution instead of list scans, neighborhood
grabs (families pulled in one motion), and composition paths read as
lattice routes. **Placement must be derived from measured relations**
(family structure, chain co-use, the symbiosis pairwise evidence —
a 9,900-pair Dell-to-Dell relation map already exists as research),
never from decoration: a lattice whose "near" means nothing would
make fast pulling pull the wrong things. The index is a *derived
reading over the one canonical registry* — never a second store
(architectural law). Open questions (owner: Director/Ace) are in the
Decision Register.
