## The vision — Ace's objectives (distilled, faithful)

1. **Proposal cards in the Nursery.** Docked-AI proposals
   presented as glanceable cards — what is proposed, what it
   touches, confirm/reject obvious. Deliberate design work, not
   an afterthought.
2. **Custom idea shapes, chosen in the Nursery.** Circle,
   square/box, triangle, or custom-drawn outline, chosen before
   commit and held as part of an idea's identity presentation.
3. **The space: ROOM (3D) and SKY (2D).** The Plane presented
   as a walkable 3D room (boxes placed, stacked, opened; 3D
   coordinates) and a 2D sky (ideas as clouds; 2D coordinates).
   Ideas stay where Ace puts them; presentation must not look
   stagnant; navigation by zoom + tap-to-glide, never stepwise
   through coordinates.
4. **Human-testing gates.** No human-testing push until (a) a
   real AI is docked live and (b) Ace has worked the Matrix
   himself; look/feel bumps mapped on paper first.
5. **AI-suggested looks, as proposals only.** Semantic icons
   and a shape language (shapes carry category meaning) may be
   suggested by the AI; suggestions land as Nursery proposals
   Ace commits or overrides. Never silent decisions.
6. **The Nursery is the most important screen.** Where users
   spend the most time and make decisions; must feel like
   painting/building — fun, organized — never an information
   dump. The Plane is second.
7. **A small, context-linked page set.** Plane; Nursery; Dells
   page; Dell Playground (hands-on Dell use); and the Psalm
   Matrix. Switching pages keeps the user inside the same idea.
8. **Navigation placement is a researched decision.** Ace
   corrected his own top-bar instinct: placement (top/bottom/
   side) for a creative spatial tool is to be decided from
   online research evidence in the future GUI work.
9. **The PSALM MATRIX is the AI page.** AIs are called Psalms.
   It holds the Persona Matrix (persona choice), shows all
   Psalms and their docking, docking happens there, and it is
   where the user talks to a Psalm in pure English through a
   form that is MORE than a chat box — a new concept to invent.
10. **RESERVED — the Director's forthcoming item.** Ace's
    preview: neural-network work, advanced, for later, aimed at
    enhancing a less powerful AI through what the Matrix gives
    it. The directive arrives from the Director through Ace;
    nothing is invented in its place.
