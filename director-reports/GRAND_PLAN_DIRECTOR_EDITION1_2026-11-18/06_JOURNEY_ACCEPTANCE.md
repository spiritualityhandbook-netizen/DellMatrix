**Acceptance grades:** NOT_RUN, PARTIAL_SCOPE, FULL_SCOPE_PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED_BY_AUThORITY, UNSUPPORTED_BY_DESIGN. A journey receives FULL_SCOPE_PASS only when all named mandatory steps for the adopted contract have executable public-path proof at the same relevant head; a future feature in the journey must be explicitly gated or it remains NOT BUILT.
