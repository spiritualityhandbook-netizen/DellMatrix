### FI-012 — Elastic Intake Doctrine

**Provenance:** director-reports/FEATURE_INTAKE_REGISTER_ADDENDUM_2026-11-17.md, originating Ace's 2026-11-15 17:22 wording; development in goal workspace. **Status:** doctrine ruled; full development reported delivered in goal workspace, product transition NOT built. **User goal:** any-size information intake, from a letter to large content, using safe demand-grown/chunked storage/processing. Internally defined caps must be visible growth/windowing/continuation points, not silent loss. At hard physics boundaries expose chunk/window/stream and state the actual consumer limitation; distinguish this target architecture from present interim validation/resource guards.

**Design:** a reader/writer stream contract {owner, operation, schema_version, chunk_seq, bytes_received, bytes_verified, total_if_known, resume_token, generation, digest, status}; validate order, no duplicate writes on retry, preserve opaque bytes until declared semantic parsing, and prevent unlimited in-memory buffering. The user can resume rather than lose or silently shorten data. A pre-existing honest hard guard is allowed as an INTERIM protector until a safe streaming implementation has replaced it; record transitional exceptions by source/head and consumer. **Proof:** 1 byte, 5,000, 100,000, multi-megabyte on representative low-memory machine; interrupted chunk, resume/replay, malicious size, disk full, stale token, cross-owner and save/load integrity. **Placement:** cross-cutting P0/P2/H7/H8, upgrades GP-E01/E02/E05 and every intake door.
