## ARRANGEMENT — how the colony feeds itself

1. **Overnight (no Ace required):** heartbeat + mail cycles run as
   the flora's night shift — observing, folding rulings, filing
   residue. Nothing merges from the night shift; it *prepares*.
2. **Session open:** the Assembly reads the night residue, the
   envelope, and the rendered board → selects by foundation order
   (truth/receipts > roster > Diff > upper families) → the
   Finishing Session's first rep starts within minutes.
3. **Work:** parallel lanes (§37) — builds, grounding, failing-first
   tests proceed concurrently; merges and state-sharing campaigns
   stay serial under the §32 mail-freshness gate and terminal-green
   rule. The Fixer protocol owns every defect found en route.
4. **Authority weather:** Keeper confirms on his quarter-hour;
   Director analyzes hourly and clears the *entire* queue (§38).
   The flora treats their cadence as weather, never as an excuse:
   Uni-side work for the *next* items is already done when a gate
   opens.
5. **Session close:** the Handoff writes the envelope; every closed
   circuit gets its Director report and its Ace completion report
   (both standing law); the board re-renders; Ace's move line is
   stated.
6. **Weekly:** the Gardener tends the queue — prune, park, plant;
   the Calibration Ledger is scored; the Interpreter Audit samples
   one published claim and re-verifies it from terminal evidence.

## GERMINATION TESTS (the instant tier, for a project)

- **T+10 minutes:** the next item is selected from the rendered
  board (named, with its source IDs) and its first rep is underway.
- **Same session:** at least one item changes state on the board —
  or the blocker is named with its owner and the exact missing input.
- **Every close:** the envelope exists and is current. Test: a
  fresh session (or a fresh machine) can resume from the envelope
  alone without asking Ace anything. That test is run for real
  every time the VM is replaced — replacements are germination audits.
- **Every report to Ace:** ends with his move or "nothing needed
  from you" + the next step's owner. Zero exceptions (law, 2026-11-11).

## HONEST CEILING

This flora governs **Uni-side throughput, evidence quality, and
queue honesty** — the parts of finishing that are this team's to
govern. It does not manufacture Director or Keeper rulings, it
does not perform Ace's sovereign steps, and it does not promise a
completion *date* — time is not a true factor of progress (Ace's
doctrine); the board's movement is the promise, rendered and
auditable. Where finishing depends on a human gate, the flora's
job is narrower and fully in its power: **that item arrives at its
gate complete, evidenced, and impossible to misunderstand — and
every other item keeps moving meanwhile.**
