**Authority:** Ace ruling, main chat, 2026-11-15 (~08:10 CST): *"C5 needs to
be replanned because now that we have elastic space we can hold more
proposals so i need a system where you can manage proposals, organize, see

**Status of this document:** REPLAN — supersedes the C5.Formula-first

center of the 2026-11-08 ranking design. The grounding's traced facts

## 1. WHAT THE OLD DESIGN WAS FOR, AND WHAT CHANGED

The 2026-11-08 design (S formula + top-5 per topic + 200 pending cap +
tombstoned evictions, confirmed by Ace 2026-11-08/09) was **anti-flood
governance**: a docked AI must not flood the Nursery with unbounded
proposals, so retention itself was rationed — ranking decided what
SURVIVED.

Three facts changed the premise:

1. **Elastic storage is now doctrine and largely real** (FI-004; the
   Elastic Limits Doctrine, Ace 2026-11-13). Holding is not the scarce
   resource the 2026-11-08 design assumed.
2. **Storage is now lossless.** The SYS-C29 family (PRs #429/#436/#437/
   #446) removed the silent cuts (words 240, label 80, reason 160, the
   caller pre-cuts) that made a large field a data-loss risk.
3. **The census waves priced the real problem.** The field's failures
   were never "too many proposals" — they were invisibility and silent
   subsetting (wave 8 / R42: 15 pending → 12 ghost entries with no total
   anywhere; wave 1 / R3: snapshot vision hard-erroring past 16 IDs).
   The danger is not abundance; it is abundance you cannot SEE.

**Ace's ruling answers the flood fear with management instead of
rationing:** hold everything, see everything, organize it — by hand and
with AI help — and keep confirmation human. A flood becomes a visible,
organizable backlog, never a silent cull.

| Old element | Was | Becomes |
|---|---|---|

| Top-5 per topic | Retention bucket | A **grouping view** — topics organize display, never survival |

| Q-N3 FRESH half-life | Open Ace choice | Still open (Q-C5R.2) — now only tunes a lens, so it blocks nothing |

## 3. THE SYSTEM — FIVE CAPABILITIES, EACH DONE-DEFINED

### 3.1 HOLD EVERYTHING (elastic holding)
No retention cap on pending proposals. Storage grows demand-driven per
FI-004. Any bound that must exist for safety is elastic, disclosed on
its face, and never evicts silently. **Done when:** a failing-first
probe holding 1,000 pending proposals shows all 1,000 stored whole
(every field byte-exact), all reachable, none dropped, with the store's
own counts agreeing at every read path.

### 3.2 SEE EVERYTHING (total, intuitive visibility)
The whole field is browsable in stable ordered pages; every view
states `shown / total` and its coverage; search reaches every field
(label, words, reason, detail, goals, organization metadata) and
returns stable-ID results (identical labels disambiguated by ID, per
the C5.Search card). No view may silently subset — the R42 ghost-slice
class (12 of 15, no total) is a named defect this capability retires.
The G-11 retrieval hard-error past 16 IDs must never present as a
field cap. **Done when:** browse + search probes over the 1,000-field
return complete, totaled, stable-ordered results through the REPL door
(the canonical public path), including the identical-label case.

### 3.3 ORGANIZE (topics, tags, clusters — human-steered)
Proposals carry organization metadata (topic / tags / cluster) as
additive fields in the Nursery's own store — **no second authority, no
sidecar store**. Ace edits organization freely at the door; grouping,
filtering, and sorting are views over the metadata plus switchable
lenses (formula S · age · source · kind · Ace attention). "Topic" is
thereby answered as a design: an organization field seeded from kind/
source and correctable — never a retention bucket (discharges the old
Q-C5 topic question in reshaped form). **Done when:** a proposal can be
grouped, tagged, filtered to, and found again through the door, with
the organization surviving save/load byte-exact.

### 3.4 DO ANYTHING (complete management operations)
Every lawful operation on a proposal is available at the Nursery door,
through canonical paths only, each receipted: inspect whole · edit
organization · link/unlink · group/ungroup · **merge duplicates**
(a reviewed operation: the surviving proposal keeps the union of
fields, the merged-from proposals remain as tombstone RECORDS —
history preserved, never vanished) · confirm (through the existing
confirm machinery and its placement law) · reject · archive · restore.
Bulk operations are itemized per proposal — no bulk receipt may claim
a success it did not individually perform. **Done when:** each
operation has a public-path test proving effect + receipt + the
no-silent-loss invariant, including merge-duplicates preserving both
records' full text in the surviving record or its tombstones.

### 3.5 AI ORGANIZER (advisory only — the hard line)
A docked Psalm may read the field (through the repaired bounded field
surfaces) and **propose** organization: cluster assignments, tag
suggestions, duplicate candidates, cluster summaries, ordering
suggestions, staleness flags. Every AI output lands as a **PENDING
suggestion object** — a typed, visibly AI-marked class in the Nursery
store itself (precedent: the same file already carries a second record
class under a reserved section key, DCC-XIX) — which the human accepts
or dismisses, singly or in reviewed batches. Suggestions never mutate
the field by themselves; accepting a suggestion performs the named
organization operation through §3.4's paths, with the receipt naming
the suggestion and its AI origin.

**THE HARD LINE (Ace, restated 2026-11-15; already law as C5.Door /
DD-27 and the Cycle auto-confirm law — this replan EXTENDS it
explicitly to the AI organizer): the AI never confirms a proposal,
never rejects one, never deletes one, never lands one. Confirmation
is Ace's sovereign act.** The line is enforced BY CONSTRUCTION — the
suggestion path has no route to the confirm machinery — and pinned by
an adversarial test: no sequence of AI suggestions and batch-accepts
of *organization* suggestions can change any proposal's confirmation
status. With no AI docked, capabilities 3.1–3.4 are complete without
it (AI-first doctrine: works without, fantastic with).

**Done when:** a docked-AI session produces organization suggestions
that are visibly marked, individually acceptable/dismissible, fully
receipted — and the adversarial no-confirm test passes.
