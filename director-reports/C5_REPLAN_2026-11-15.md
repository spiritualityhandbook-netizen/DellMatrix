# C5 REPLAN — The Nursery Proposal Management System

**Authority:** Ace ruling, main chat, 2026-11-15 (~08:10 CST): *"C5 needs to
be replanned because now that we have elastic space we can hold more
proposals so i need a system where you can manage proposals, organize, see
everything intuitively, do anything in the nursery without missing data or
limits and ai helping to organize and manage them never confirming things
automatically."*

**Status of this document:** REPLAN — supersedes the C5.Formula-first
scoping proposed in the C5 grounding (PR #444) and re-scopes the scarcity
center of the 2026-11-08 ranking design. The grounding's traced facts
stand unchanged (the confirmed formula was never implemented; the live
ranker is the superseded recipe; no topic field, no cap, no tombstone
exists in code). What changes is the DESIGN CENTER and the slice order.
Build starts per-slice on Director authorization (plan-complete gate,
Grand Workflow).

## 1. WHAT THE OLD DESIGN WAS FOR, AND WHAT CHANGED

The 2026-11-08 design (S formula + top-5 per topic + 200 pending cap +
tombstoned evictions, confirmed by Ace 2026-11-08/09) was **anti-flood
governance**: a docked AI must not flood the Nursery with unbounded
proposals, so retention itself was rationed — ranking decided what
SURVIVED.

Three facts changed the premise:

1. **Elastic storage is now doctrine and largely real** (FI-004; the
   Elastic Limits Doctrine, Ace 2026-11-13). Holding is not the scarce
   resource the 2026-11-08 design assumed.
2. **Storage is now lossless.** The SYS-C29 family (PRs #429/#436/#437/
   #446) removed the silent cuts (words 240, label 80, reason 160, the
   caller pre-cuts) that made a large field a data-loss risk.
3. **The census waves priced the real problem.** The field's failures
   were never "too many proposals" — they were invisibility and silent
   subsetting (wave 8 / R42: 15 pending → 12 ghost entries with no total
   anywhere; wave 1 / R3: snapshot vision hard-erroring past 16 IDs).
   The danger is not abundance; it is abundance you cannot SEE.

**Ace's ruling answers the flood fear with management instead of
rationing:** hold everything, see everything, organize it — by hand and
with AI help — and keep confirmation human. A flood becomes a visible,
organizable backlog, never a silent cull.

## 2. WHAT SURVIVES FROM THE OLD DESIGN — DEMOTED, NOT DELETED

| Old element | Was | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Formula S = 0.40 LINK + 0.25 ACE + 0.20 FRESH + 0.15 NOVEL | Retention gatekeeper | One **ordering lens** (the default) — decides what you look at first, never what exists |
| Top-5 per topic | Retention bucket | A **grouping view** — topics organize display, never survival |
| 200 pending cap | Retention bound | **Abolished as a retention bound.** Survives at most as a display page size, always shown next to the true total |
| Tombstoned evictions | Overflow disposal | **Abolished** (nothing is evicted). Tombstones remain only as the *record* form of human merge/reject operations |
| Aging marks 3/7/14, fade at 30 (MWL 177) | Expiry pressure | **Visual signals only** — staleness highlighting; a quiet or aged proposal stays fully reachable, confirmable, and intact |
| Q-N3 FRESH half-life | Open Ace choice | Still open (Q-C5R.2) — now only tunes a lens, so it blocks nothing |

## 3. THE SYSTEM — FIVE CAPABILITIES, EACH DONE-DEFINED

### 3.1 HOLD EVERYTHING (elastic holding)
No retention cap on pending proposals. Storage grows demand-driven per
FI-004. Any bound that must exist for safety is elastic, disclosed on
its face, and never evicts silently. **Done when:** a failing-first
probe holding 1,000 pending proposals shows all 1,000 stored whole
(every field byte-exact), all reachable, none dropped, with the store's
own counts agreeing at every read path.

### 3.2 SEE EVERYTHING (total, intuitive visibility)
The whole field is browsable in stable ordered pages; every view
states `shown / total` and its coverage; search reaches every field
(label, words, reason, detail, goals, organization metadata) and
returns stable-ID results (identical labels disambiguated by ID, per
the C5.Search card). No view may silently subset — the R42 ghost-slice
class (12 of 15, no total) is a named defect this capability retires.
The G-11 retrieval hard-error past 16 IDs must never present as a
field cap. **Done when:** browse + search probes over the 1,000-field
return complete, totaled, stable-ordered results through the REPL door
(the canonical public path), including the identical-label case.

### 3.3 ORGANIZE (topics, tags, clusters — human-steered)
Proposals carry organization metadata (topic / tags / cluster) as
additive fields in the Nursery's own store — **no second authority, no
sidecar store**. Ace edits organization freely at the door; grouping,
filtering, and sorting are views over the metadata plus switchable
lenses (formula S · age · source · kind · Ace attention). "Topic" is
thereby answered as a design: an organization field seeded from kind/
source and correctable — never a retention bucket (discharges the old
Q-C5 topic question in reshaped form). **Done when:** a proposal can be
grouped, tagged, filtered to, and found again through the door, with
the organization surviving save/load byte-exact.

### 3.4 DO ANYTHING (complete management operations)
Every lawful operation on a proposal is available at the Nursery door,
through canonical paths only, each receipted: inspect whole · edit
organization · link/unlink · group/ungroup · **merge duplicates**
(a reviewed operation: the surviving proposal keeps the union of
fields, the merged-from proposals remain as tombstone RECORDS —
history preserved, never vanished) · confirm (through the existing
confirm machinery and its placement law) · reject · archive · restore.
Bulk operations are itemized per proposal — no bulk receipt may claim
a success it did not individually perform. **Done when:** each
operation has a public-path test proving effect + receipt + the
no-silent-loss invariant, including merge-duplicates preserving both
records' full text in the surviving record or its tombstones.

### 3.5 AI ORGANIZER (advisory only — the hard line)
A docked Psalm may read the field (through the repaired bounded field
surfaces) and **propose** organization: cluster assignments, tag
suggestions, duplicate candidates, cluster summaries, ordering
suggestions, staleness flags. Every AI output lands as a **PENDING
suggestion object** — a typed, visibly AI-marked class in the Nursery
store itself (precedent: the same file already carries a second record
class under a reserved section key, DCC-XIX) — which the human accepts
or dismisses, singly or in reviewed batches. Suggestions never mutate
the field by themselves; accepting a suggestion performs the named
organization operation through §3.4's paths, with the receipt naming
the suggestion and its AI origin.

**THE HARD LINE (Ace, restated 2026-11-15; already law as C5.Door /
DD-27 and the Cycle auto-confirm law — this replan EXTENDS it
explicitly to the AI organizer): the AI never confirms a proposal,
never rejects one, never deletes one, never lands one. Confirmation
is Ace's sovereign act.** The line is enforced BY CONSTRUCTION — the
suggestion path has no route to the confirm machinery — and pinned by
an adversarial test: no sequence of AI suggestions and batch-accepts
of *organization* suggestions can change any proposal's confirmation
status. With no AI docked, capabilities 3.1–3.4 are complete without
it (AI-first doctrine: works without, fantastic with).

**Done when:** a docked-AI session produces organization suggestions
that are visibly marked, individually acceptable/dismissible, fully
receipted — and the adversarial no-confirm test passes.

## ADDENDUM 2026-11-15 — ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (Director condition, EXE DELTA mail 1a1dfd2eec764e2c)

The Director accepted the replan's design intent and required acceptance
criteria BEFORE implementation. These criteria are the pass conditions for
each slice. A slice is DONE only when every criterion is demonstrated by a
registered suite or a public-path probe at the slice's own head, with the
evidence in the slice's circuit packet. Criteria are stated against the
REPL door first (the canonical public path); other surfaces inherit.

### C5R.1 HOLD — nothing is ever lost
- AC-1.1 Failing-first probe: 1,000 pending proposals, each with maximal
  admitted fields (words 5,000, label 200, reason 1,000), are stored and a
  fresh process reload returns all 1,000 with every field byte-exact.
- AC-1.2 Every read path (list, ranked, browse, ghost/all-entities) reports
  counts that AGREE with the store's true count; any view that shows a
  subset states shown/total on its face (the R42 defect class is closed).
- AC-1.3 No code path in the Nursery store deletes or evicts a proposal as
  a side effect of capacity; the only removals are the receipted human
  operations of C5R.5.
- AC-1.4 The SYS-C29 family bounds hold unchanged (words/label/reason
  refusal-before-mutation), verified by the family suites on the slice head.

### C5R.2 SEE — browse and search everything
- AC-2.1 Paged browse over ALL proposals (pending + confirmed + archived)
  with a stable order: two identical queries return identical ID sequences;
  page N+1 continues exactly where page N ended (no repeats, no skips),
  proven at 1,000 proposals.
- AC-2.2 Search matches against every stored field (label, words, reason,
  detail, goals, origin/metadata) and returns stable proposal IDs, so
  identical labels never collapse two proposals into one result.
- AC-2.3 Every view states shown/total and its filter; an empty result
  states the searched total (never a silent empty screen).
- AC-2.4 Retrieval by ID set works for any count (the G-11 16-ID hard
  error never presents as a field cap); partial failure returns the found
  set plus the missing IDs named.

### C5R.3 ORGANIZE — topics, tags, clusters as metadata
- AC-3.1 Topic/tags/clusters live as additive metadata in the Nursery
  store itself (no second store, no sidecar file); a save/load round trip
  preserves them byte-exact alongside the proposal fields.
- AC-3.2 Seeding from kind/source is automatic and receipted; human
  correction of any organization field is a receipted edit that never
  alters the proposal's content fields.
- AC-3.3 Grouping views (by topic, by tag, by cluster) partition or cover
  the field exactly as their filter states, with shown/total per group.

### C5R.4 LENS — ranking as an ordering, never a gate
- AC-4.1 The S formula (0.40 LINK + 0.25 ACE + 0.20 FRESH + 0.15 NOVEL)
  is implemented as the DEFAULT lens with every component's signal source
  named in code and packet; a missing signal is shown as missing, never
  silently zero-filled without disclosure on the receipt.
- AC-4.2 Every ranked view displays score + components per proposal.
- AC-4.3 Ranking NEVER gates existence, reachability, or permission:
  the lowest-ranked proposal is as retrievable, inspectable, and
  confirmable as the highest (probe: confirm the last-ranked proposal).
- AC-4.4 Lenses are switchable (at minimum: S-default, newest, oldest,
  alphabetical); switching changes order only, never the set.

### C5R.5 MANAGE — the full operations set, receipted
- AC-5.1 Operations at the door: inspect-whole, edit-organization,
  link/unlink, group/ungroup, merge-duplicates, confirm, reject,
  archive, restore — each producing a receipt naming actor, operation,
  proposal IDs, and before/after state.
- AC-5.2 Merge-duplicates: the survivor carries the UNION of both
  proposals' fields and organization metadata; the merged-from proposal
  remains as a tombstone record (never a silent deletion), and the
  operation is reversible by restore.
- AC-5.3 Bulk operations are itemized: the receipt lists every affected
  proposal and its individual outcome; a partial failure reports exactly
  which items failed and why, with the succeeded set intact.
- AC-5.4 Confirm follows the standing placement law unchanged (Ace's
  mixed-wall choice asked each time; auto placements stage in the
  sandbox first).

### C5R.6 AI ORGANIZER — proposes organization, never confirms
- AC-6.1 A docked Psalm's organization output lands ONLY as PENDING
  suggestion objects of a typed, visibly AI-marked class in the Nursery
  store (cluster assignments, tag suggestions, duplicate candidates,
  summaries, ordering suggestions, staleness flags).
- AC-6.2 Accepting a suggestion performs exactly the named C5R.5
  organization operation, with the receipt naming the suggestion ID and
  its AI origin; dismissing removes the suggestion with a receipt.
- AC-6.3 THE HARD LINE, adversarial: NO sequence of AI suggestions and
  batch-accepts of organization suggestions can change any proposal's
  confirmation status, reject or delete any proposal, or land anything
  on the Plane. A registered adversarial suite attempts the escalation
  paths (suggestion-to-confirm, batch-accept chains, malformed
  suggestion payloads) and every attempt is refused by construction —
  the suggestion path has no route to the confirm machinery (verified
  by caller trace in the packet, not only by test).
- AC-6.4 With NO AI docked, C5R.1–C5R.5 are complete and fully usable
  (AI-first doctrine: the organizer is an addition, never a dependency).
