## Questions for the Director
Q1. ASCII-only count grammar: non-ASCII decimal digits (Arabic-Indic, fullwidth, superscripts, circled) are refused/defaulted rather than normalized and converted. Does that match the intended public grammar? Q2. The 9-digit bound: any label longer than 9 ASCII digits is refused before conversion, even though every valid Loop count clamps to 1..5 anyway. Is a grammar bound (rather than convert-then-clamp) the intended reading of "avoid unbounded conversion for huge counts"?
