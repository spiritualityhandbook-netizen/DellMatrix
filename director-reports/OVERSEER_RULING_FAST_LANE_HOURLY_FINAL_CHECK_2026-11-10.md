## Rule (refines AGENTS.md S34 harmony rule)

1. Pre-agreement between the two analysis authorities is NOT a
   precondition for progress. Differing proposals, analyses, or
   preferences do not freeze work.
2. The fast lane executes: within existing authorization, Uni
   proceeds on the best-supported course. Grok's CONFIRM
   suffices to move — including merges under the hard gate and
   the S32 freshness check — provided NO HOLD /
   CORRECTION_REQUIRED / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE from either
   authority is outstanding on that item.
3. The Director's HOURLY cycle is the real final check.
   Everything executed since the last check is reviewed there,
   and the check must pass WITH EVERYTHING IN AGREEMENT (both
   authorities' positions and Uni's evidence aligned) for the
   work to stand as finally verified and closed.
4. A disagreement surfaced at the hourly check binds
   immediately from that point: the item freezes, and anything
   requiring correction is corrected through the normal circuit
   path. Every fast-lane action leaves the complete decision
   trail that makes this auditable and correctable.
5. A HOLD from either authority still binds the instant it is
   issued, at any time, in either lane.
6. Certification remains agreement-based. A fast-lane merge is
   an execution under standing authorization, recorded as such,
   pending the hourly final check. Merged never means
   certified.
