## Rule (refines AGENTS.md S34 harmony rule)

1. Pre-agreement between the two analysis authorities is NOT a
   precondition for progress. Differing proposals, analyses, or
   preferences do not freeze work.
2. The fast lane executes: within existing authorization, Uni
   proceeds on the best-supported course. Grok's CONFIRM
   suffices to move — including merges under the hard gate and
   the S32 freshness check — provided NO HOLD /
   CORRECTION_REQUIRED / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE from either
   authority is outstanding on that item.
3. The Director's HOURLY cycle is the real final check.
   Everything executed since the last check is reviewed there,
   and the check must pass WITH EVERYTHING IN AGREEMENT (both
   authorities' positions and Uni's evidence aligned) for the
   work to stand as finally verified and closed.
4. A disagreement surfaced at the hourly check binds
   immediately from that point: the item freezes, and anything
   requiring correction is corrected through the normal circuit
   path. Every fast-lane action leaves the complete decision
   trail that makes this auditable and correctable.
5. A HOLD from either authority still binds the instant it is
   issued, at any time, in either lane.
6. Certification remains agreement-based. A fast-lane merge is
   an execution under standing authorization, recorded as such,
   pending the hourly final check. Merged never means
   certified.

## ADDENDUM — EITHER AUTHORITY CERTIFIES; BOTH EVENTUALLY; NOTHING WAITS

Ace / Overseer, 2026-11-10 ~03:46 CST (relayed in chat,
verbatim): "Grok can certify and so can gpt but they both
should eventually.. nothing should stop progress no waiting
on anything to continue work."

1. EITHER authority may certify. A certification by Grok (fast
   lane) is as effective when given as a certification by the
   Director (hourly lane), and vice versa.
2. BOTH should EVENTUALLY certify: the second authority's
   certification is a trailing obligation, tracked on a
   pending-second-certification list in the SUSX state file
   and cleared as it lands. It is never awaited.
3. NOTHING WAITS: no work item stalls pending a second
   certification, a trailing review, or any agreement step.
   Progress continues on the first effective certification
   (plus the hard gate and S32 where they apply).
4. A trailing disagreement is handled like an hourly-check
   disagreement: it binds from the moment it is issued, the
   item freezes, and correction runs through the normal
   circuit path on the complete decision trail.
5. HOLD / CORRECTION_REQUIRED / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE remain
   stop orders, not certifications: they bind instantly
   whenever either authority issues one. A conditional hold
   discharges when an authority certifies its stated condition
   is met.
6. First applications: C1.PulseReceipt / DD-18 closes on the
