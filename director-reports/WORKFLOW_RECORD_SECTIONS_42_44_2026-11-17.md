## §42 — EXE = FULL CAPACITY EVERY CYCLE (Ace ruling, 2026-11-16 08:13 CST)

1. A cycle is a progress CHECK, not an assignment slot: EXE =
   do everything in your capacity that cycle. Idle capacity
   beside workable work is a defect. The only lawful "nothing
   can progress" is an absolutely full plate.
2. Authority cadence: Director EXEs twice per hour (:00 final
   check, :30 delta); Keeper EXEs twice per hour (:15 fast
   confirm, :45 deep pass) — four authority EXEs per hour. Each
   EXE covers EVERYTHING ripe in that authority's capacity that
   cycle.
3. Uni EXEs on the 2-minute watch tick as a progress check and
   on the 30-minute execution cycle as full execution. Merges
   and state-sharing campaigns remain serial main-session acts.
4. The Director PLANS: he reads the codebase and AUTHORS PLANS
   (his production lane, since he cannot write code). A Director
   plan enters the same pipeline as any plan — checked against
   the codebase and the Grand Plan, confirmed and stamped
   through the normal authority path.
5. The Keeper's expanded lane: useful work including code-shaped
   drafts in his own lane, fenced by non-interference with Uni's
   simultaneous coding (separate lane, separate files, no shared
   mutable state) and by the same checking standards as
   everything. (Write authority itself is governed by §43.)
6. Role flex within the fences. Fences that never move: Ace's
   authority; HOLD binds instantly; merged never means
   certified; only Uni merges.

## §44 — EMAIL SUNSET RULE (Ace ruling, 2026-11-16)

Once the GitHub-first workflow (§43 reassessment/design) is
built and adopted, email leaves the transport lane entirely.

1. Exception-only: email may be used only when it is absolutely
   useful AND itself creates progress the GitHub record cannot
   (a genuinely external party, a channel-failure fallback, or a
   notice whose only useful path is mail).
2. Drop the rest: routine ruling/report transport, duplicate
   notifications, mail versions of things already on GitHub, and
   any mail handling that costs time or energy without creating
   progress.
3. Until adoption: existing mail folding remains only as the
   legacy fallback for rulings that have not yet moved to
   GitHub; it shrinks as the new workflow lands and is never
   expanded. (Operational note, 2026-11-17: the switch is HELD
   while Workflow v2 adoption is pending on PR #461; the mail
   digest is retained permanently as the rollback for connector
   outages.)
