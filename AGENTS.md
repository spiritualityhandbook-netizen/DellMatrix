## BIMO

BIMO is the multi-agent fusion body. Dock personas into slots, then fuse:

```text
you> bimo defaults
you> bimo fuse
you> matrix personas
you> personas
```

## Commands

```text
personas                 full roster
matrix personas          spatial persona matrix
persona <name>           activate lens (+ dock to BIMO slot)
guide                    active persona guidance
bimo | bimo defaults | bimo fuse | bimo dock <slot> <persona>
workshop persona | workshop bimo
```
